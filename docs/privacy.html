Privacy Policy

Exhausted or Nauseous (“EON”) · Last updated: August 2, 2026

This policy describes how Exhausted or Nauseous (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you use our mobile app.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used to create and sign in to your account via SMS one-time codes.
  • Profile information — first name, last initial, username, and optional profile photo.
  • Status / emotion — when you tap Exhausted or Nauseous, that status is shared with your accepted friends.
  • Push notification token — used to send you notifications when friends update their status or send friend requests.
  • Friend connections — friend requests and friendships you create in the app.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Show your profile and status to friends you connect with
  • Notify you about friend activity
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the app

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Profile details and status are visible to friends you accept. We use service providers to run the app (for example, hosting, authentication, and push delivery). Those providers process data only to provide their services to us.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email doelgern@gmail.com from the phone number on your account and request deletion.

Children

Exhausted or Nauseous is not directed to children under 13.
